## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the tactile, scholarly gravitas of an ancient tabletop codex re-engineered for precise modern gameplay. Blending classical antiquarian parchment textures with crisp, game-ready interface mechanics, it evokes the solemn weight of mythic campaigns, dice rolls, and ancient tactical grids.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile / Skeuomorphic** accents with modern structured editorial design:
- Physical metaphors drawn from inscribed tablets, ink-stamped ledgers, and beaten brass medallions.
- High functional clarity: stat blocks, combat logs, and roll meters sit on layered parchment tiers with distinct chiselled borders.
- Mythological tri-pantheon accents: Greek emerald for life and stamina, Norse runic cyan for mana and fate manipulation, and Egyptian solar gold for divine favors, crits, and prime action mechanics.
- The interface feels ancient yet surgically clear—parchment that has resisted centuries, penned with deep obsidian and sepia ink.

## Colors

The palette reproduces aged vellum illuminated with sacred inks and metallic regalia. High contrast between obsidian inks and bright vellum ensures swift readability during active encounters.

### Base Parchment Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#F6EFE2` (Aged parchment / warm marble foundational backplane).
- **Surface Lower:** `#DACDB4` (Recessed wells, inset trays, roll troughs).
- **Surface Default:** `#EDE4D3` (Standard cards, stat sheets, character sheets).
- **Surface Raised:** `#E3D8C3` (Hover states, elevated module frames, side drawers).
- **Surface Highlight / Crisp Sheet:** `#FBF7EE` (Selected tabs, active spell/combat scrolls, popover surfaces).

### Ink & Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Ink (Obsidian/Bronze):** `#2A2218` (High-contrast titles, core values, dice totals).
- **Secondary Ink (Deep Sepia):** `#4A3E31` (Labels, body text, combat modifier descriptions).
- **Muted Ink (Aged Umber):** `#6E5F4E` (Footnotes, passive traits, disabled states).
- **Filigree Border / Inscription Rule:** `#C4B49A` (Subtle ornamental rules, compartment dividers).

### Mythic Tri-Engine Accents
- **Egyptian Solar Gold (Primary):** `#B8860B` (Bright accent: `#C9A24B`, Deep Brass base: `#8C6508`). Primary actions, critical hits, major resource counters, and brass bevels.
- **Greek Sage / Emerald (Secondary):** `#2E7D32` (Lighter tone: `#388E3C`, Deep tone: `#1B5E20`). Health, active stances, survival rolls, positive stat differentials.
- **Norse Rune Cyan (Tertiary):** `#0288D1` (Lighter tone: `#0097A7`, Deep tone: `#01579B`). Aether pools, fate tokens, 2d6 modifier mechanics, arcane checks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges historical authority with mathematical legibility:

- **Display & Headings (Domine):** Sturdy, chisel-inspired serif that mirrors etched runestones and illuminated manuscript titling. Used for chapter headers, entity names, major outcomes, and card titles.
- **Body & Longform Descriptions (Work Sans):** Rational, humanist grotesque with neutral clarity. Provides effortless reading for extensive spell rules, combat mechanics, and inventory descriptions without stylistic fatigue.
- **Data, Stats & Dice Formulae (Space Grotesk):** Engineered geometric grotesque with proportional numerals. Handles roll formulas (`2d6 + 4`), target defense values (DC), initiative brackets, cooldowns, and tactical chips with crisp precision.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a structured 12-column grid on desktop/tablets, consolidating into a dense single-column tactical stack on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margin, `1.25rem` gutters. Accommodates the three-panel layout: Character Sheet / Dossier (left 3 cols), Interactive Combat Grid & Dice Pit (center 6 cols), and Action/Log Ledger (right 3 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Ledger collapses into an off-canvas drawer or lower tab sheet.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margin, `0.75rem` gutter. Dice roller and quick-action bar remain pinned to the bottom viewport.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8px base rhythm (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 36px) ensuring tightly aligned stat matrices and parchment module dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons modern synthetic blurs in favor of layered physical tiers, hand-carved insets, and embossed metallic edges.

1. **Recessed Plane (Level -1):**
   - Inset shadows: `inset 0 2px 4px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.12), inset 0 0 0 1px #C4B49A`.
   - Used for the dice-rolling pit, locked skill sockets, and text input fields.
2. **Parchment Base (Level 0):**
   - `#F6EFE2` flat background with fine border accents: `1px solid #E3D8C3`.
3. **Card/Scroll Layer (Level 1):**
   - Surface `#EDE4D3` accompanied by a soft, warm ambient drop shadow: `0 2px 6px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.04)`. Outlined with a continuous hairline border of `#DACDB4`.
4. **Interactive Raised Tiles (Level 2):**
   - Surface `#FBF7EE` with dimensional cast shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.12), 0 2px 4px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.06)`. Top highlight edge: `1px solid #FFFFFF`.
5. **Mythic Metallic Overlay (Level 3 - Medallions & Floating Modals):**
   - Deep brass/gold cast: `0 8px 24px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.18), 0 3px 6px rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.15)`. Border: Dual compound borders (`1px solid #B8860B` surrounded by an offset `1px solid #8C6508`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry (`0.25rem` standard radius, `0.5rem` for large containers, `0.75rem` for modals and central parchment rolls).

This subtle curvature emulates sheared vellum, heavy stock cardboard tokens, and trimmed brass corner-brackets without looking overly modern or bubbly.
- Small indicators, dice faces, and input tags: `rounded` (`4px`).
- Encounter cards, equipment sheets, and dossiers: `rounded-lg` (`8px`).
- Modal scrolls and master trays: `rounded-xl` (`12px`).
- Corner ornamentations: Select card corners utilize notched or chamfered clip-paths or inset gold dots (1px brass rivets) in the padding space.

## Components

### Buttons & Actuators
- **Primary Brass Action (Roll 2d6 / Commit Action):**
  - Gradient background: `linear-gradient(180deg, #C9A24B 0%, #B8860B 60%, #8C6508 100%)`.
  - Foreground text: `#FBF7EE` with `text-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
  - Border: Embossed effect via `border-top: 1px solid #F6EFE2`, `border-bottom: 2px solid #5A4105`, and `border-inline: 1px solid #8C6508`.
  - Active/Pressed: Inset shadow `inset 0 3px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)` with 1px downward translation.
- **Secondary Mythic Buttons:**
  - *Greek Stance (Emerald):* `#2E7D32` with `#388E3C` top bevel.
  - *Norse Runic Cast (Cyan):* `#0288D1` with `#0097A7` top bevel.
  - *Subtle Parchment Action:* Background `#EDE4D3`, border `1px solid #C4B49A`, text `#2A2218`. Hover shifts to `#FBF7EE`.

### Cards & Character Dossiers
- Constructed on `#EDE4D3` with internal compartments on `#FBF7EE`.
- Outlined with a continuous 1px `#DACDB4` border, bracketed by a delicate secondary internal keyline `3px` inside the perimeter.
- Headers feature Domine bold with an ornamental gold or bronze hairline horizontal rule extending to the container's trailing edge.

### 2d6 Dice Tray & Numeric Counters
- **Dice Nodes:** Square tiles with `rounded` (4px) corners, surface `#FBF7EE`, deep obsidian pips or numbers (`#2A2218`), bordered with an inscribed brass stroke (`1.5px solid #B8860B`).
- **Critical & Hazard Pips:** 6s highlighted in Norse cyan or Egyptian gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 8px rgba(184, 134, 11, 0.4)`); natural 1s highlighted with deep umber/crimson tint.

### Chips & Stat Modifiers
- Tight capsules constructed with `Space Grotesk` uppercase text.
- Standard Modifiers: Background `#DACDB4`, ink `#2A2218`.
- Buff / Boon Chips: Background `#E8F5E9` (light sage), text `#1B5E20`, border `1px solid #2E7D32`.
- Arcane / Fate Chips: Background `#E1F5FE` (light cyan), text `#01579B`, border `1px solid #0288D1`.

### Inputs & Combat Log Fields
- Background `#DACDB4` with inset bevel shadow (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(42, 34, 24, 0.15)`).
- Text in `#2A2218`, placeholder in `#6E5F4E`.
- Focus state: Solid outer highlight `1.5px solid #B8860B`, background transition to `#FBF7EE`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom square markers with an engraved bronze bezel.
- Unchecked: Inset `#DACDB4` with `#C4B49A` border.
- Checked: Core filled with Egyptian Solar Gold `#B8860B` containing a deep obsidian `#2A2218` runic cross or checkmark.
